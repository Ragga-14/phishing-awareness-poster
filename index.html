Think Before You Click

Phishing is the #1 way attackers get into organizations. One click can expose everyone's data.

1. What is phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where a criminal pretends to be someone you trust (your bank, IT department, boss, or a delivery company) to trick you into clicking a malicious link, opening an attachment, or giving away passwords and data.

Email phishing
Spear phishing (targeted)
Smishing (SMS)
Vishing (voice calls)

2. How the attack works

  1. 1Bait: attacker sends a convincing message.
  2. 2Pressure: urgency or fear ("Your account will be closed!").
  3. 3Click: victim opens a fake link or attachment.
  4. 4Steal: credentials or malware are captured.
  5. 5Breach: attacker enters the organization's network.

4. How to protect yourself

STOP

Pause. Does it create urgency or ask for sensitive info?

CHECK

Verify the sender address and hover over links before clicking.

VERIFY

Contact the sender using a known number or official website.

REPORT

Forward to IT/security and delete. Never reply.

ENABLE MFA

Multi-factor authentication limits damage if a password leaks.

UPDATE

Keep your system, browser and antivirus up to date.
